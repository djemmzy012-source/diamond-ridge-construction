Corporate Overview

Engineering Global Landmarks Since 2001.

Diamond Ridge Engineering Group is one of the world’s leading vertical engineering and commercial construction corporations, recognized for delivering large-scale skyscraper developments, institutional infrastructure systems, and multi-billion-dollar urban engineering projects across international markets.

Built on Vision. Driven by Engineering.

Founded in 2001 by Alejandro Thomas, Diamond Ridge Engineering Group began as a specialized structural engineering company focused on high-rise commercial developments throughout North America.

Under the leadership of Alejandro Thomas, the corporation rapidly expanded into international markets, establishing operations across Asia-Pacific, the Middle East, and Europe while becoming recognized for complex vertical engineering systems and enterprise-scale construction execution.

Today, Diamond Ridge Engineering Group oversees billions of dollars in active developments worldwide, employing thousands of engineers, project managers, procurement specialists, and construction professionals across multiple continents.

The corporation remains committed to innovation, precision engineering, environmental sustainability, and uncompromising construction standards while continuing to redefine modern metropolitan skylines around the world.
